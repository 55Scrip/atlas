# ASML Research Notes

## Thesis Notes
- Lithography leadership remains the central thesis.
- Extreme ultraviolet (EUV) is a critical enabler for advanced semiconductor nodes.
- Customer concentration (TSMC, Samsung, Intel) is a structural feature of the market, not a hidden risk.

## Evidence Gaps
- Margin durability through a downcycle has not been reviewed recently.
- China revenue exposure needs updated context following recent export control developments.
- Long-term service revenue contribution relative to equipment revenue not yet assessed.

## Open Questions
- What assumptions about EUV demand durability should be rechecked before changing decision status?
- Which financial history quarters are most relevant to reviewing margin durability?
- Has the thesis on customer concentration risk changed since the last review?

## Risks to Monitor
- Export controls affecting China shipments.
- Customer capex cyclicality during semiconductor inventory corrections.
- Competitive dynamics if alternative lithography approaches develop.

## Reason to Wait
- Evidence gaps on margin durability and China exposure remain unresolved.
- No new financial review has been completed since the last journal entry.
