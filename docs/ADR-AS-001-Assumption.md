# ADR-AS-001 — Assumption

**Status:** Accepted. Validated per `docs/ADR-AS-001-Validation-Report.md` (Accept with Changes; changes incorporated).
**Type:** Ontology ADR — Atlas Core, Decision Workspace domain. Produced by the ADR Adoption Program, Sprint 6 (Wave 2), converting recommendations from `docs/ADR-Investigation-007-The-Nature-of-Assumptions.md` and `docs/ADR-Investigation-008-Assumption-vs-Hypothesis-vs-Conclusion-vs-Judgment.md`, following the process established by `ADR-GOV-001`, `ADR-GOV-002`, `ADR-GOV-003`. Depends on `ADR-CC-001-CaseCondition.md`.

---

## Problem

UX-009 requires Assumptions to be individually named, confirmed, and tracked for support/challenge over time as part of the reasoning behind a recorded Decision. No existing object satisfies this, and the codebase already, confusingly, uses the word "Assumption" for an unrelated concept.

## Context

`Investigation-007` tested nine candidate framings (belief, expectation, prediction, dependency, hypothesis, accepted uncertainty, accepted proposition, premise, condition, claim) and found an Assumption is fundamentally a proposition the investor's or Atlas's reasoning treats as true, without independently re-proving it, such that the reasoning's own soundness depends on the proposition remaining true — synthesizing "premise" (the logical role) with "dependency" (why it matters). It also surfaced a genuine, previously-undiscovered naming collision: `OutlookAssumption` (`atlas/analysis_engine/outlook.py`) already uses the word "Assumption" for a fully structured, fully numeric, Atlas-owned valuation-model-input disclosure with no free-text "why" field — a different concept sharing only a name, the third instance of naming overload this document series has found (after "Reflection" and "Evaluation"). `Investigation-008` then tested `Investigation-007`'s conclusion genuinely, not defensively, against `Hypothesis`, `Evidence`, `Conclusion`, and `Judgment` — all five read fresh or reconfirmed from entity code — and independently reconfirmed it via differently-angled substitution testing, while also discovering that the implemented `atlas/core/` Core Loop and the separate "Atlas Reasoning Foundations" track are, per the already-Accepted `ADR-005`, explicitly not governing or converged with one another; this ADR treats the implemented objects as authoritative throughout, exactly as every prior investigation in this series has.

This ADR converts `INV7-R1` through `INV7-R4`, `INV7-R6`, `INV7-R8`, `INV7-R9`, and `INV8-R1`, `INV8-R3`, `INV8-R7`, consistent with `Atlas-Recommendation-Register.md`'s own dependency graph. `INV7-R7` (the `DE-005` integration point) and `INV8-R4`/`INV8-R5`/`INV8-R6` (the C-02 authorship-gap disclosure, the no-silent-type-change invariant, and the Evidence-direction invariant, respectively) are reflected in Rationale, Consequences, and Invariants below rather than as separate Decision points, since none of the twelve topics this ADR's own governing Sprint specified names them directly.

## Decision

1. **Adopt `Assumption`** — a stable, Decision-anchored identity (with `case_id` reachable transitively, following every comparable object in this series) representing a single premise's statement: free text, Atlas-proposable, investor-confirmable/editable.
2. **Truth is never tracked as a binary verdict for `Assumption`.** Only "currently supported" vs. "currently challenged" is tracked — never true/false/settled/rejected — consistent with this codebase's own anti-false-precision doctrine. Whether the assumption was reasonable to hold *at the time* is a separate, historically-fixed property from whether it currently remains supported.
3. **`Assumption` and `Hypothesis` remain distinct objects, never merged.** `Hypothesis` is reasoning-independent — Atlas assigns it no truth value, confidence, or conviction at all, the weakest epistemic commitment in the family — an unresolved candidate explanation still being tested. `Assumption` is reasoning-dependent by definition — a premise the current, active reasoning already stands on. The same statement text can be either, depending entirely on whether the investor is currently *testing* it (Hypothesis) or currently *relying* on it (Assumption); this is an epistemic-stance difference, not a wording accident.
4. **Evidence supports or challenges an Assumption's underlying claim — it never proves, replaces, or destroys it.** This is the calibrated verb this codebase's own Evidence/Counter-Evidence structure already uses; `Evidence.direction` (`SUPPORTS`/`CHALLENGES`) is the mechanism, and it is a required, structural field carrying a direction, never a verdict.
5. **`Assumption` and `Conclusion` remain distinct objects, never merged.** `Conclusion` is mandatorily anchored to exactly one `Evidence` record and is the *output* of an evidence-weighing process; `Assumption` is closer to an *input* the reasoning starts from or stands on — a different direction in the reasoning chain. Something concluded earlier may later be relied upon as a premise, but this is always a new `Assumption` capture informed by the prior `Conclusion`, never a mutation or transformation of the `Conclusion` itself.
6. **`Assumption` and `Judgment` remain distinct objects, never merged.** `Judgment` is a settled, Case-wide characterization, independent of any specific reasoning chain — it needs no supporting Decision to remain relevant. The distinguishing test: does the proposition's own relevance depend on a specific, active piece of reasoning (Assumption), or does it stand as a general characterization regardless of any specific reasoning (Judgment)? A real, evidence-grounded distinction, confirmed against worked examples, not a wording accident.
7. **Assumptions are extracted from, or stated alongside, a Decision's own `reason` — a derivative sibling, never stored on `Decision` itself.** `Decision` depends structurally on `reason`; `reason`, accumulated across a position's Decision history, constitutes Thesis (derived, per `DE-005`, never stored separately); `reason`/Thesis implicitly rests on zero-or-more individually-nameable Assumptions.
8. **`Assumption` and `CaseCondition` (`ADR-CC-001`) remain a loose, optional cross-reference — never merged, never one contained by the other.** Not every Assumption needs an evaluation lifecycle; forcing all of them into `CaseCondition`'s own predicate-evaluation shape would be architecturally wasteful and semantically imprecise for the likely-majority no one is actively tracking. Where `CaseCondition` does target something in this epistemic family, it should primarily, though not exclusively, target `Assumption` rather than `Hypothesis`, `Conclusion`, or `Judgment` — the tightest, most well-motivated fit among the four.
9. **`Assumption`'s lifecycle is leaner than an eight-state model.** "Draft" is not a state of `Assumption` itself — if content originates from `DecisionDraft`, "Draft" belongs to that separate, upstream object. "Accepted" is the creation event, not a separate state. "Supported" is a projection, not a stored state — the default, ongoing relationship absent any challenge event. "Challenged" and "Invalidated" collapse toward one event type, differentiated by degree, not a truth verdict. "Retired" is a distinct terminal event, independent of whether the evidence relationship changed. "Rejected" (an Atlas-proposed candidate the investor never accepted) describes something outside `Assumption`'s own lifecycle entirely. "Deleted" is genuinely unresolved — the same open question this series has already left open for `DecisionDraft` and `CaseCondition`.
10. **`Assumption`'s lifecycle MUST use the identical event-stream pattern established for `CaseCondition` (`ADR-CC-001` §2) — a stable identity plus append-only revision/challenge/retire events**, the fourth reuse of the Security-Confirmation-derived pattern in this document series.
11. **The existing `OutlookAssumption` (`atlas/analysis_engine/outlook.py`) must never be conflated with `Assumption` in any future naming, documentation, or code.** No existing object — `Decision`, `DecisionContext`, `ReflectionResponse`, `Evaluation`, `Learning`, `Observation`, `Question`, `Conclusion`, `Judgment`, `KnowledgeReference`, `ReasoningTrace`, `Hypothesis`, or `OutlookAssumption` — satisfies the ontology established above.
12. **The following models are rejected:** `Assumption` as free text on `Decision.reason` (loses individual identity, trackability, and challenge history entirely); `Assumption` as `CaseCondition` (forces an evaluation lifecycle onto content that doesn't inherently need one); `Assumption` as a `KnowledgeReference`/Knowledge object (wrong shape — reference-based, not proposition-based); a linear pipeline with Assumption "before" Hypothesis (no such enforcement exists anywhere in the implemented Core Loop); Assumption as an accepted Hypothesis with no distinction after acceptance (the epistemic-stance difference persists after acceptance, per §3); Judgment as an accepted Conclusion (fails on two independent structural grounds — a different pair than Assumption/Hypothesis, retained here since `Investigation-008`'s own Phase 18 tests it alongside this family); a minimal ontology treating Assumption and Judgment as presentation-only (contradicts both the `DE-005` integration this series has found and Judgment's own normative status in the separate Domain Object Architecture track).

## Rationale

`Investigation-007`'s conclusion is not merely adopted by default — `Investigation-008` genuinely re-tested it against every plausible substitute from a differently-angled direction and found it independently reconfirmed, the strongest evidentiary basis any single-object recommendation has received in this series. Keeping all five epistemic-family objects (`Assumption`, `Hypothesis`, `Evidence`, `Conclusion`, `Judgment`) distinct, rather than merging any pair, follows directly from testing each merge attempt to a specific, structural failure — not from a general preference against consolidation. Reusing `CaseCondition`'s own event-stream pattern (§10) rather than inventing a new one is deliberate, consistent with this program's own governing discipline of treating complexity as something discovered through demonstrated necessity, never introduced speculatively.

## Alternatives Considered

See Decision §12, above, for the specific rejected models and their individual grounds.

## Consequences

- `Decision`'s immutability, and everything downstream that relies on it, remains completely untouched.
- No existing object in the epistemic family (`Hypothesis`, `Evidence`, `Conclusion`, `Judgment`) requires modification; all four remain unwired to Alpha, unchanged by this ADR.
- `DE-005`'s own existing Decision Memory synthesis text already anticipates exactly this kind of decomposed, per-position reasoning content — a genuinely strong, already-anticipated future integration point, stronger than any comparable Atlas Memory hook this series has found elsewhere, though not designed or performed by this ADR.
- A disclosed, real inconsistency is inherited, not resolved, by this ADR: `Assumption` is the only member of this five-object epistemic family integrated with `ADR-002` C-02's authorship-transfer model; extending that model to the other four is a real future question this ADR does not decide.
- A real, disclosed integration-discipline risk exists between `Assumption` and `CaseCondition`: an Assumption could be retired while a `CaseCondition` still watching it is never told, letting the two drift out of sync — named, not resolved, since resolving it would mean designing the actual cross-reference mechanism.
- The "evaluate" naming-collision risk, now confirmed across the whole epistemic family (Core Loop `Evaluation`, `CaseCondition`'s own evaluation process, and here, evaluating an Assumption's underlying claim), must be actively managed by any future implementation's own naming choices.

## Invariants

- `Assumption` itself is created once and never mutated; every subsequent change is a new event in its own stream (`revised`, `challenged`, `retired`, `superseded`).
- "Supported" is always a derived projection, never a stored state.
- Truth is never tracked as a binary verdict — only "currently supported" vs. "currently challenged."
- No object in this epistemic family ever silently changes type — a `Hypothesis` "becoming" an `Assumption`, or a `Conclusion` "becoming" a `Judgment," is always a new capture, never a mutation.
- `Evidence` carries a required direction (`SUPPORTS`/`CHALLENGES`) relative to whatever it bears on, never a verdict, across every object it might inform.
- The name `Assumption` (this concept) and `OutlookAssumption` (the existing, unrelated Atlas-methodology object) must never be conflated in code, comments, or product copy.
- Unedited acceptance of an Atlas-proposed assumption follows `ADR-002` C-02's authorship model exactly — never silently relabeled as user-authored.

## Migration

None to existing code — no `Assumption` object exists yet, and `Hypothesis`, `Evidence`, `Conclusion`, and `Judgment` are unmodified by this ADR. A future implementation effort would build `Assumption` and its event stream against this ADR's own shape.

## Open Questions

- Whether the `Assumption`/`CaseCondition` cross-reference should be enforced (a `CaseCondition` watching a retired Assumption is flagged) or left entirely loose — not resolved here, restated unchanged from `Investigation-007`.
- Whether "Rejected" (an Atlas-proposed candidate the investor never accepted) is worth its own event type, or falls entirely outside `Assumption`'s own lifecycle — genuinely unresolved.
- Whether `OutlookAssumption` should be renamed to close the naming collision, now documented three times over across this series — a documentation/naming question for a future, separate task, not decided here.
- What the precise mechanism is by which a future `Assumption` feeds `DE-005`'s own thesis-strength synthesis — genuinely promising, not designed here.
- Whether the `ADR-002` C-02 authorship-transfer model should eventually be extended to `Hypothesis`, `Evidence`, `Conclusion`, and `Judgment` — a disclosed gap, no urgency established, not decided here.
- Whether provider-synchronized Evidence honestly satisfies "the investor regarded this as evidence," and what happens to this whole family's "investor's own account" authorship framing if Atlas itself ever originates a Hypothesis or Conclusion — both genuinely open, not resolved by this ADR.
- Whether the OE-002/Reasoning-Foundations track and the implemented `atlas/core/` objects should ever be reconciled, and whose definition of Judgment would govern — explicitly not authorized by `ADR-005`, not decided here either.
- The same conversion-path question raised in `ADR-DC-001`'s own Open Questions applies identically here.
